I am a theoretical physicist working on non-equilibrium statistical physics, stochastic processes, and soft matter. I use analytical models, scientific computing, and machine learning to study interacting systems.

My research has covered ionic transport and correlations in electrolytes, the glass transition in polymer films, granular flows, and the structure of random networks.

I received my PhD in Physics from Université PSL at ESPCI Paris, in the Gulliver Laboratory, and subsequently carried out postdoctoral research at Tel Aviv University and IPhT Saclay.

I am looking for research roles in AI and scientific machine learning.

Scientific machine learning

I built a PyTorch study of electrolyte dynamics, comparing causal GRUs, regression, and a physical model reduction. The models can predict currents accurately while missing slow relaxation.
